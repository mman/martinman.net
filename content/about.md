I'm Martin Man, a software engineer who spends a lot of time in a
Niesmann+Bischoff iSmove 6.9E motorhome.

This site collects notes on making the van smarter and more self-sufficient:
Victron Energy power systems, Node-RED automations, exposing the whole thing
to Apple HomeKit, and the custom electronics it takes to glue it together.

Everything here is written in English. Articles are grouped into
[series](/series/) when they belong to a multi-part project.
